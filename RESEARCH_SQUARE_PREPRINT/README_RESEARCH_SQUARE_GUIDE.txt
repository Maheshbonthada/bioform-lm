FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Q: Will submitting to Research Square prevent Bioinformatics from accepting it?
A: No. Most journals allow preprints. Bioinformatics explicitly allows posting
   on preprint servers before and during peer review.

Q: Will I get in trouble for posting a preprint?
A: No. Over 90% of biomedical research is preprinted before journal publication.

Q: Can I update the preprint after submitting?
A: Yes, Research Square allows "versioning" — you can upload revised versions.

Q: How long does Research Square take to publish?
A: Usually 24-48 hours for review. Publication is fast (you typically see it
   within 1-3 days).

Q: Will the preprint DOI stay the same if I update it?
A: Yes. A new version gets a new version number but same DOI. This is standard.

Q: How do I link my preprint to the Bioinformatics submission?
A: When submitting to Bioinformatics, include the Research Square DOI/link in
   the cover letter (optional but helpful).
