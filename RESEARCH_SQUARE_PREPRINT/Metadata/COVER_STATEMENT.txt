This manuscript presents BioForm-LM, a novel generative AI system for biologics
formulation design. We are posting this preprint to establish priority for our
work and enable immediate community feedback, while the manuscript undergoes
peer review at Bioinformatics (Oxford University Press).

Key contributions:
• First generative model for de novo biologics formulation design
• Novel three-stage architecture combining mechanistic simulation + in-context
  learning + physics-informed decoding
• BioFormBench: curated open benchmark of 67 real biologics formulations
• Demonstrated perfect calibration and 35.3% coverage on leave-one-protein-out
  evaluation with only 3-10 in-context examples

This preprint is simultaneously under consideration at Bioinformatics journal,
a peer-reviewed venue. Preprint publication does not preclude journal
publication - it aligns with modern scientific practice in computational
biology and machine learning.

Generated recipes are computational hypotheses for wet-lab screening and should
not be construed as clinical or commercial recommendations.
